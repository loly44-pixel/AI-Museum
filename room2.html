AI Around Us.

Artificial intelligence is not only something we see in science fiction. It already helps people in different fields. Explore these three examples to discover how.

EXHIBIT 01
01

Healthcare

AI can help doctors examine medical images and find patterns that may be difficult to notice.

How AI helps
AI systems can analyze medical images, such as X-rays, and highlight areas that may need a closer examination by a healthcare professional.

Why it matters
It can support doctors in making informed decisions. AI does not replace the doctor's judgment.
EXHIBIT 02
02

Education

AI can help students learn by explaining difficult topics and adapting practice activities.

How AI helps
AI learning tools can provide explanations, examples, and practice questions based on a student's needs.

Why it matters
Students can use it to review lessons, understand mistakes, and practice at their own pace.
EXHIBIT 03
03

Navigation

AI helps navigation systems understand traffic patterns and suggest better routes.

How AI helps
Navigation systems can use traffic information and patterns to estimate travel times and suggest alternative routes.

Why it matters
It helps people plan journeys and may reduce time spent in traffic.
WHAT I DISCOVERED

AI is already part of everyday life. From helping doctors examine images to supporting students and improving navigation, AI can assist people in different ways. The important part is understanding how to use it responsibly and checking its results.
