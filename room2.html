BEYOND THE SCREEN

AI in Real Life.

Artificial intelligence is not just something we see in movies. It is already part of our everyday lives. Explore three ways AI helps us in the real world.

🩺

Healthcare

AI helps doctors analyze medical images, detect possible diseases, and support medical decision-making.

AI can help identify patterns in X-rays and medical scans. Doctors still review the results and make the final decisions.
🚗

Transportation

AI helps navigation apps predict traffic, suggest faster routes, and support the development of self-driving cars.

AI systems can analyze traffic conditions and road information to help people make better travel decisions.
🎓

Education

AI can personalize learning, explain difficult concepts, and help students practice new skills.

AI learning tools can adapt explanations and practice activities to a student's needs, while teachers guide the learning.
THINK ABOUT IT

AI is already around us.

From the moment we wake up to the moment we go to sleep, artificial intelligence can quietly support our daily activities.

The question is not whether AI will affect our lives, but how we choose to use it.
