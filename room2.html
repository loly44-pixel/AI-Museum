ROOM 02 · AI IN OUR WORLD

AI Around
Us

Artificial intelligence is not just a futuristic idea. It is already part of our everyday lives. Explore three ways AI helps people around the world.

✧
EXHIBIT 001

Healthcare

AI can help doctors analyze medical images and identify patterns that may be difficult to notice.

AI tools can support doctors when examining X-rays and other medical images. They can help identify areas that need closer attention, but medical professionals remain responsible for diagnosis and care.
✎
EXHIBIT 002

Education

AI learning tools can explain difficult topics and help students practice at their own pace.

AI can provide practice questions, explain concepts in different ways, and give feedback. It can support learning, but students still need to think critically and check the information.
⌖
EXHIBIT 003

Navigation

AI helps navigation applications estimate traffic and suggest alternative routes.

Navigation systems can analyze traffic patterns and road information to estimate travel times. This helps people make better decisions about their journeys.
