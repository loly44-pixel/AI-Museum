EXHIBIT 004 · MY CREATIVE PROCESS

How AI Helped Me

I used ChatGPT as a creative assistant to brainstorm ideas, organize information, and help develop the HTML and CSS for my digital museum. It helped me turn my concept into an interactive website with separate exhibition rooms.

From Idea to Museum

First, I chose the idea of creating a digital museum about artificial intelligence. Then, I organized the experience into four exhibition rooms. I used AI to help develop the website structure and interactive features. Finally, I reviewed the content and customized the design to create my own digital experience.
