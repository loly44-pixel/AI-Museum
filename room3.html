EXHIBIT 003 · A SURPRISING DISCOVERY

AI Can Learn Patterns

Before exploring AI, I thought computers could only follow instructions written by humans. What surprised me is that some AI systems can learn patterns from examples and use those patterns to make predictions or generate new content.

A Tiny Experiment

EXAMPLE 01
A picture of a cat is labeled "cat".
EXAMPLE 02
Another picture of a cat is also labeled "cat".
EXAMPLE 03
A new picture appears. Can a trained AI model recognize that it may also be a cat?

The Unexpected Part

A trained AI model may recognize a new cat image because it learned patterns from its training examples. It does not need to memorize every single cat picture. However, its prediction can still be wrong, especially when the new image is very different from the examples it learned from.

"AI can learn from examples, but learning patterns is not the same as understanding the world like a human."
